2. CEO Dashboard
The CEO Dashboard is the main management screen.
It should provide a high-level financial snapshot first, with the ability to drill down into detailed reports.
2.1 Main KPI Cards
The dashboard should display the following cards:
Total Purchase Cost
Shows the total amount spent purchasing properties during the selected period.
Example:
Total Purchase Cost: PKR 125,000,000
Calculation:
Sum of all property purchasing costs within the selected period.

Total Selling Revenue
Shows the total amount received/contracted from property sales.
Example:
Total Selling Revenue: PKR 175,000,000
Calculation:
Sum of all property selling amounts within the selected period.

Gross Profit
Gross profit before operating expenses, taxes, commissions, salaries, etc.
Gross Profit = Total Selling Revenue − Total Purchase Cost
Example:
Selling Revenue: PKR 175M
Purchase Cost: PKR 125M
Gross Profit:
PKR 50M

Total Agent Commission
Shows total commission paid/payable to agents.
Example:
Agent Commission: PKR 5,000,000

Total Office Expenses
Includes normal business operating expenses.
Examples:
Office rent
Electricity
Internet
Telephone
Stationery
Marketing
Transportation
Maintenance
Office supplies
Software subscriptions
Cleaning
Security
Other office expenses

Total Employee Salaries
Shows salaries paid during the selected period.
Example:
Employee Salaries: PKR 2,500,000

Total Tax Paid
Shows taxes paid by the business.
Example:
Tax Paid: PKR 3,000,000

Total Zakat Paid
Shows Zakat paid during the selected period.
Example:
Zakat Paid: PKR 1,000,000

Total Other Expenses
Includes expenses that do not fall into the predefined categories.

Net Profit
Net profit should be calculated after all applicable business expenses.
Recommended calculation:
Net Profit = Gross Profit − Agent Commission − Taxes − Zakat − Salaries − Office Expenses − Bills − Other Expenses
Example:
Gross Profit = PKR 50M
Less:
Agent Commission = PKR 5M
Salaries = PKR 2.5M
Office Expenses = PKR 1.5M
Tax = PKR 3M
Zakat = PKR 1M
Other Expenses = PKR 1M
Net Profit:
PKR 36M

32. Important Financial Rules
The system should keep the following values separate:
Purchase Cost
Actual cost of acquiring the property.
Selling Revenue
Revenue generated from selling the property.
Gross Property Profit
Selling price minus property cost.
Operating Expenses
Business expenses such as salaries, rent, marketing and utilities.
Agent Commission
Commission paid/payable to agents.
Tax
Government/business tax obligations.
Zakat
Zakat should be separately recorded and reported.
Net Profit
The final profit after applicable costs and expenses.

37. Final Objective
The objective of this module is to give the CEO a complete real-time picture of the company's financial health.
The CEO should not need to manually calculate:
Purchase totals
Sales totals
Property value
Profit
Agent commissions
Salaries
Office expenses
Bills
Taxes
Zakat
Other expenses
Cash flow
The system should calculate these automatically from the underlying transactions.
Core Formula
Total Sales
minus
Total Property Purchase Costs
equals
Gross Profit
Then subtract:
Agent Commission
Salaries
Office Expenses
Bills
Marketing
Property Expenses
Taxes
Zakat
Other Expenses
equals
NET PROFIT
The CEO dashboard should provide both summary information and complete drill-down details, so every figure can be traced back to the original transaction.
